# Revdo Design Direction

Revdo Studios is an independent design and software studio. The visual direction should feel editorial, precise, calm and technically competent. It should not look like a generic AI landing page.

## Identity

- Brand: Revdo Studios
- Mark: minimalist RD monogram
- Location: Bandung, Indonesia
- Domain: design, digital product, websites, custom software and technology systems

## Visual Language

- Editorial studio website, not SaaS template.
- Strong typography, generous composition and restrained color.
- Real imagery and generated assets should look like work, studio, product or material references. Avoid abstract filler.
- Cards are allowed for work items, but page structure should not become a repeated card grid.

## Palette

- Ink: `#0e0e0c`
- Bone: `#ede8df`
- Parchment: `#d9d2c2`
- Rust accent: `#a8421c`
- Moss secondary: `#3d4a3a`
- Fog muted: `#8f897d`

Use rust as the deliberate accent. Do not spread it everywhere.

## Typography

- Serif headings for studio/editorial character.
- System sans for readable body text.
- Monospace only for small labels and operational metadata.

Reason: the contrast between serif headings and restrained sans body gives the studio a crafted identity without relying on decoration.

## Dials

- ENERGY: 2
- RHYTHM: 2
- MOTION: 1

The site should feel considered and premium, with section variation but no heavy animation.

## Avoid

- Purple or blue AI gradients
- Decorative orbs, fake terminal windows, fake dashboards and generic status dots
- "AI powered", "next generation", "seamless", "revolutionary" and similar filler
- Fake statistics, fake testimonials and invented client logos
- Dead links or placeholder social proof

## Decision Reasons

- Color: earth-toned studio palette supports a design-and-software brand without the common AI-tech palette.
- Layout: single-page routing supports a simple portfolio and admin-managed content without requiring a framework.
- Typography: serif headings give editorial character, sans body keeps readability.
- Imagery: WebP assets make the work visible instead of replacing it with abstract decoration.
- Admin CMS: file-based editing is enough for a small studio site and keeps deployment simple on a VPS.
